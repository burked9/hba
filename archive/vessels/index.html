Heritage Vessels Register & Archive

The information on vessels was first gathered in 2006 for the book Cool Metal – Clear Water and expanded in Fine Lines – Clear Water. This archive preserves all 105 canonical entries across 11 categories from the Joomla register, plus 32 early static HTML pages (2004–2007) and 42 WordPress overview pages (2020–2026).
