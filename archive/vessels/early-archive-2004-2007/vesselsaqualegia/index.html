HERITAGE BOAT ASSOCIATION "Celebrating Ireland's Floating Heritage"
Aqualegia Aqualegia is a replica of a Dutch Luxemotor and has been specifically built to travel the inland waterways of Ireland and France. She was designed and built to have all the strengths of the original Luxemotors, with some modern additions and replacements. Like the originals she has a round stern with pointed bow, a cabin to the rear, engine room with dismountable wheel house on top, a steel bulkhead in front of the engine room and where the deep hold would have been, is further living space. Under the water her hull is a double-ender (pointed at both ends) making it ideal for cruising inland waterways as she handles well in confined waters and creates virtually no wash to disturb the banks and wildlife. The interior was inspired by vessels of the 1920s with painted tongue and groove trimmed with varnished wood. Since she does not carry freight, there is no need for a mast and boom but ballast is required, which is a combination of brick and water tanks. She has a quiet Beta Marine keel cooled engine which occupies about one fifth of the space needed for the original Brons and Kromhouts of the 1920s.
