The aspiration of the **Heritage Boat Association (HBA)** is to protect, promote, and celebrate the floating heritage of the inland waterways of Ireland. What remains of our Floating Heritage provides a direct link to the past, including both commercial and pleasure craft that plied the inland waterways through the different eras of the canal, lake, and river systems.

### About This Digital Preservation Project
This archive preserves the complete historical vessel register, crew reminiscences, boatbuilder histories, and photographs compiled by HBA members over the past 25 years—including foundational research from *Cool Metal – Clear Water* (2006 / 2017) and *Fine Lines – Clear Water* (2009)—across all three historical editions of `heritageboatassociation.ie`:

1. **The 2004–2007 Static HTML Edition** (`/*.htm` & `/HBASiteSpring2007/*.htm`)
2. **The 2008–2019 Joomla CMS Edition** (`/cms/index.php?...`)
3. **The 2020–2026 WordPress Edition** (`/vessels/...` & `/essential_grid/...`)
