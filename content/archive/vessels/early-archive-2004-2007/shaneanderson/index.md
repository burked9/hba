**New Assistant Inspector Waterways Ireland**

Lieutenant Commander (NS) Retd. Shane ANDERSON has recently taken up the post
of Assistant Inspector of Navigation with Waterways Ireland. He will be part of
the Waterways Inspectorate team, his principal role being the enforcement of the
various navigation bye-laws on the inland waterways under the remit of Waterways
Ireland. He will also carry out inspections of the navigations and of vessels in
relation to the safety.

Shane is presently operating from the Waterways Ireland offices and Depot in
Tullamore. He will have principle responsibility for the Grand Canal, Royal
Canal and Barrow navigation.

Shane comes from a naval background with 27 years experience in the Irish
Naval Service which included a number of command positions before he retired and
moved to Bruxelles with his family where he worked with the European Commission
on enforcement issues relating to fisheries in European waters.

We wish Shane all the best in his new appointment on the calm waters of the
inland navigation.
