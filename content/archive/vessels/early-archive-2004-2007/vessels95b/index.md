|  | |  | | --- | | **History Of Canal Boat 95B**                     Back to [Vessels](vesselb.htm) | | Name of Boat: 95B  Type/Style of Boat: Barge  Year of Build: 1919  Length/Beam/Draft: 60 x 13 x 3   Weight:- 45 Tons  Noteable features: Canal Boat 95B commenced its life transporting Malt barley from Athy to Guinness Brewery in Dublin under the ownership of the Barrow transport company. From 1956 to the closure of the canal in 1960 it was used for mixed cargo including carrying sugar beet to the factory in Carlow. Typical load would have been 50 Tons with a draught of just over 4 feet. After the canal closure it found it way into the ownership of the construction company building the new bridge in Youghal on which it ran aground after completion of the bridge. It lay in its watery grave for approximately 5 years until purchased and rescued by George Spears who refloated the vessel and brought it back to Athy where she as converted to an early house boat in the late 60s.  95B was originally powered by an Bolinder 15HP semi diesel single cylinder engine that was only removed in 1986 and replaces with the current 6 cylinder Perkins which is still in situ.  The vessel is currently having a complete refit which includes new decks, superstructure and bow.  Autumn 2004: 95B currently in Dry Dock Tullamore undergoing major refit.
