Boatmens Reunion 2008 a Great Success
29 May 2008

Garrykennedy on Lough Derg was the focus of the Boatmens's Reunion 2008.

A broad range of members of the HBA and various Heritage Boats were in attendance at the Boatmen's Reunion 2008

Events included a meal and gathering in Larkins, and a presentation by CEO of Waterways Ireland, Mr. John Martin.

Gerry Burke from the HBA expressed thanks to members of the HBA who had made the trip across Lough Derg in inclement weather.

"We should never forget that it was the hard work and toil of these men who kept these boats on the water for decades, when the Irish inland navigation system was at its height in terms of transport and cargo delivery. The HBA have worked with the Boatmens Reunion over the past few years to ensure the rich heritage and memories of the working days of these large old craft is kept alive"

The HBA wish the Boatmen and their families well and look forward to at next year's annual gathering and as many visits in between as can be managed.
