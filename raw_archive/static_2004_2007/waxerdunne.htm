Paddy Waxer Dunne, Allenwood.

It is with regret that we report the passing of Paddy Waxer  Dunne, from Allenwood, who was noted as one of the great Gentlemen of the Grand Canal.

Waxer joined the Grand Canal Company as a second greaser in May 1932 serving with his father and brother on 48M. He later worked his way through 13M, 69M, 65M, 42M, 68M, 53M, 43M, 59M, 74M, 62M. At the closure of the canals in 1960 he remained on to skipper one of the last 14 working boats that wrapped up the Guinness contract.

He served 27 Years on the canals, but was a dedicated bargeman for life. Waxer attended the Canalmens reunion in Daingean earlier this year giving the barge fleet a thorough inspection and recounting some of their history to the present skippers. He took great pleasure in seeing their restoration and revival of his former charges.

Waxer has made a positive impact on all those who had the pleasure of his company. He account of his canal experiences in the book  Through the Locks gives a unique insight of his life and experiences on the barges together with the comradery of the bargemen.

While we are saddened by the news of his passing but we are confident that he will be be keeping an eye on the fleet from above.

Sean OReilly,

Chairman, HBA
