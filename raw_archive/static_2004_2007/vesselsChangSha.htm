Chang Sha
at the Ballycommon Rally, Grand Canal, Offaly.

June 2004

Brief History:

1846 Built for Mr. Sankey, said to be a director of GCC. Boat known as "Sankey's Steamer"
c1900 Sold to Major Lloyd. Steam Engine removed.Boat given the name "Chang-Sha"
1920 Sold to Department of Agriculture and used as a fisheries research station, moored at Portumna Bridge
1923 Sold to Dr. Vincent Delany, father of IWAI founder. No Engine fitted. Towed by "La Vague" to regattas etc.
1942 Sold to Syd Shine (Currently owns "Fox").2 Cyl. Kelvin engine fitted and later V8 Thornycroft.
1957 Sold to Alan Dunne and was later vandalised and sank.
c1965 Sold to Ken Simmonds.
c1970 Sold to Dan Hanovick
c1973 Sold to Simon Crowe of "Boomtown Rats"New superstructure fitted.
1980 Sold to John & David McFarlane, William Prentice, David Browne & Garry Laird.Interior Re-fitted. Perkins S6M installed.
1988 Sold to Colin & Genevieve Becker. Bilges re-plated. Interior re-fit commenced 2001.

Notes:
There are two explanations for the name "Chang-Sha". It is said that she was given the name by Major Lloyd after his return from China at the end of the 19th century. The name may mean "River House" in some Chinese dialect. Alternatively the name may refer to the capital city of the Hunan Province. The Chinese script on the cabin sides refers to the latter.
And finally, she may be a canal boat, but Chang-Sha is not a barge. She was never used as a trading boat and prefers to be referred to as a Gentleman's Steam Yacht
