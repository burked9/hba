Welcome to our images of our activities on Water Heritage Day 12005, 10th September. To celebrate our activity during the summer on the "3 Sisters" we held a photo exhibition in Graiguenamanangh Boat Club. Thanks to the Boat Club and the town groups who supported the exhibition.
 



95B lines up outside Graigue Boat Club.

Our images were divided into five themes: Vessels, People, Events, Natural Heritage and Built Heriage. Each theme had its own presentation board with up to 10 photos in each category. Some quotes were also used to lighten up the presentation.

 

  

 

We also had older and other photos available for viewing from CDs on computers. Here some of the original boatmen who live in Graigue came to visit and filled us in on some of the names and boat descriptions missing from our catalogues.



Cllr Anne Phelan kindly opened the exhibition for us and was presented with a Heritage Boat Association Plaque and a copy of our recent book ' Floating Heritage on the River Barrow' by our vice-chairperson Mr. Gerry Burke.

 

We gave talks and info sessions to people who gathered at the exhibition. Below are some of the group that walked from St. Mullins to Graiguenamanagh to culminate at the exhibition (the walk was organised in conjunction with the Barrow Branch of the Inland Waterways Association of Ireland).
