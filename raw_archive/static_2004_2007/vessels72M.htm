History Of Canal Boat 72M                  Back to Vessels
 72M in working days in James St Harbour, Grand Canal Dublin

Present owners Ronnie and Mary Byrne.

Bought from Site Investigation in July 1998 in a very derelict state.

Had been unused for 9 years.

Restoration work started immediately.

Super Structure designed and built by Richard and Mary Swayen.

First movement under power for 15 years was to join the
Dublin Rally, May 2004.
