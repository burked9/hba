 Barge Spotting (initiated by the HBA for Water Heritage Day 2004) can be undertaken from a variety of vantage points - bridges, lakes, marinas, locks, rivers and canals. Currently, barges can be found at such diverse locations at Killalloe and MountShannon on Lough Derg, Athlone, Shannon Harbour, Portumna and the Grand Canal.
Four Heritage Boat Association craft were on display for Water Heritage Day 2004: 41M at Killaloe on Lough Derg - see info page.

Also on Lough Derg where 68M and Heritage Boat ESB 2 (built in Hamburg in 1926 & brought to Ireland in 1927 for the building of Ardnacrusha) brought a Cargo of 1,000 bales of straw from Dromineer to Willamstown - log and photos here

On the Grand Canal, between Tullamore and Daingean  2.Dabu was available all day to answer questions while 95B and her owner  were  in the dry dock in Tullamore Harbour and also available for viewing.

Info on these and other HBA craft can be viewed on the vessels page.
