Boats and barges at Ballycommon last 16/17th April 2005.

Sat 16th and Sunday 17th April 2005: The HBA have travelled to Ballycommon for the last few years to mark the beginning of the dry line of the Grand Canal from Ballycommon to Kilbeggan as part of the Offaly IWAI/HBA/Kilbeggan rally. This year Kilbeggan Grand Canal Harbour Amenity Group organised a re-enactment of the digging of the Canal line and invited participants to dress in 1830 ish costume to partake in activties during Saturday afternoon.

On Saturday evening a BBQ was enjoyed by one and all till the wee hours. Sunday morning brought a bit of a canal clean up to mark An Taisce Spring Clean Month.

On a more serious note participants of the weekend took part in the unveiling of a commemorative seat to the late Heather, Mike and Maeve Thomas at Cappincur. Heather and Mike operated Celtic Canal Cruisers and did some wonderful work in helping keep open the west end of the Grand Canal.
