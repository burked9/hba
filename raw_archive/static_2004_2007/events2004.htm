 May 2004: 
Sat 1st: 4E, 34B, 68M & The Bat - Robertstown to Sallins.

Sun 9th: Descend into Dublin.

Sun 30th: Move to Edenderry possibly picking up the Little Knocknagow on the way. Leave boats for the week.
JUNE
