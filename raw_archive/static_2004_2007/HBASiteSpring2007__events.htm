The HBA Projects for 2007:
 

Support to the Year of the Barrow -  including attendance at the the Boatman's Reunion in Carlow in April, the Bagenalstown Festival in early May and the Athy Water Festival in late May.
 

Various member attendance at the Ballycommon Challenge (June) the Fleadh Ceoil (August) on the Grand Canal.
