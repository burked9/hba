Welcome to some of our general photos from our trip to the Three Sisters over the Summer. The Three Sisters system encompass the Suir, the Nore and the Barrow rivers. All have substantial history of being cargo and trade routes. The HBA were especially interested in visiting them during summer of 2005 while visiting the Tall Ships festival in Waterford.
