Welcome to some of our photos from our visit to the Athy Water Festival 27-29th May 2005. Thanks to the Athy Water Festival Committee and all involved  for making us very welcome.
 
Left: Crowds came to take part in the Festival and to look at the Heritage Boats.
 

Right: The HBA fleet made a colourful flotilla for appreciation from both land and water.
 
Left: The Athy Town Museum hosted an multimedia exhibition on the HBA throughout the festival. Talks and info sessions re HBA were also presented and tours of some boats for visitors were also permitted.
 
Right: Not only water heritage but other industrial heritage - a monorail steam train provided to the Athy Water Festival by a visiting group from Wales.
