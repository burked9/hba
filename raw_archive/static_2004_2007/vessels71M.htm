History Of Canal Boat 71M                   Back to Vessels

This photo is related to when Liam Coveny had just raised 71M from the bottom in Athy.



 

71M was bought by Betty Williams (Daughter of Harry Rice) for £125 in 1958 from CIE and used to transport grain & fertilizer to and from her farm on the Island in Waterford estuary to the Mill in Waterford and New Ross.

Betty sold 71M in 1966 to Waterford Harbour Board who allowed her to sink on a sandbank. She then passes on to the New Ross Harbour Board who again abandoned her on a sandbank in the river.

Somehow she got to Athy and was refloated by Liam Coveney who shortly after sold her to Tom Stewart.

Tom converted her at the jetty near Leeson St in Dublin and we subsequently bought her in 1987

Ó 2004 Brian Crawford, Skipper, 71M
