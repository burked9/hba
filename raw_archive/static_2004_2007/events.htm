HBA make it to Grand Canal Dock, Dublin May 2004

2004: Talk and Slide Show for Carlow County Museum 'Boatmen on the Barrow Exhibibion; Promotion of Barge Spotting for Water Heritage Day Sept 11 during Heritage Week, Scarriff Harbour Opening 01 August, Kilbeggan Challenge, and Grand Canal celebrates 200 years (1804-2004) 4E, 34B, 68M & Dabu, 36M, 56M, 74M, 95B, 72M, The Bat, Cirrus, Little Knocknagow, Chang Sha, 41M & 35M and other Heritage Boat Association vessels and friends of the HBA did some or all of the Grand Canal 2004  trip over April, May & June 2004.
