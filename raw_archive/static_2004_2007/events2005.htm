November 18-21st - Some HBA members to head off on group trip to Amsterdam. See Report from 2004 Trip to Venice here

October: Sunday 23rd, lots of members attend our AGM, a busy itinerary was discussed, report in next newsletter. The HBA Young Skipper award was presented to two brothers who spent a lot of their time on the group trip down the Barrow helping out when and whereever necessary. Congratulations to Enda and Cahal Buckley from boat Sin É, based in Tullamore.

Others head to visit new Carrick on Suir jetties .SUMMER 2005: Recent media features included radio on http://www.southeastradio.ie and many South East newspaper including features such as that in Waterford Today.

See the HBA Photo and Article in the Waterford Today newspaper, click here.
