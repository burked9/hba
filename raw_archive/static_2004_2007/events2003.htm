HERITAGE  BOAT  ASSOCIATION

"Celebrating Ireland's Floating Heritage"

   
 
 
 
 

 


 

The 2003 agenda of the HBA included a trip from Limerick north through the system to visit Carrick on Shannon, Enniskillen and Belleek. The Erne itinerary as below was kindly arranged by fellow compatriots (mainly Helen Elliott who developed the activities list below) from the Northern Ireland contingent of the Heritage Boat Association, some of whom are also members of the NI branch of the IWAI.

SUNDAY 3RD AUGUST NI Branch members met up with us at Quivvy Marina around noon. Several M Boats move to entrance to the Ulster Canal to demonstrate to all, "THE MISSING LINK" in the system. Bonfire and sing along in the evening back at Quivvy Marina by kind permission of David Cole the owner.

Places of interest visited during the following week Sun 3rd - Fri 8th:
1. Crom Castle. This is well worth a visit. The old castle and grounds are owned by the National Trust. There is a visitors centre and lots of interesting walks, The Earl of Erne still lives in the castle.
2. Share Holiday Centre. Please remember mooring charges apply. The Share Centre is a non profit making charity which was established to promote integration between physically handicapped and able bodied persons. They do excellent community work and deserve support. There is a swimming pool and other leisure facilities.
3. Inish Rath Island. This is home to the NI contingent of the Hare Krisna movement. In August, the island is open from 11.00am to 4.00pm. The telephone number is 028 6772 1512 and their web site is www.krishnaisland.net They will inform you about mooring, entrance fee, etc. They have a video presentation about the island and can arrange guided tours. There is a visitors centre serving pizza, ice-cream,cakes etc and goods sourced in India are offered for sale.
4. Inishcorkish Island serves food and the owner John Reihill may be persuaded to entertain with the fiddle!
5. Knockninny. Children's play area, toilets etc.
6. Lace Museum at Bellnaleck (beside Sheelin Restaurant).

FRIDAY 8TH ;THE MOORINGS, BELLNALECK. For years this was the home of IWAI in NI.

9.00pm: dinner kindly sponsored by Waterways Ireland.

SATURDAY 9TH APRIL This was the only early start !!!! Leave The Moorings at 10.15 am sharp to sail down in close convoy to the idyllic setting of the Killyhevlin Hotel where local media - newspapers, radio and TV were in place to greet the flotilla. A few hundred metres beyond the hotel at the Ardowen Theatre, "the theatre by the lakes", a reception for us was hosted by Fermanagh District Council.

After the reception we set off for the short trip to the island town of Enniskillen to tie up at the Round O.

1.Members of the public were invited to view the barges at close hand and talk to the skippers. Complimentary glass of Guinness available. 2. Free complimentary tickets provided by Fermanagh District Council for under 16's to use the swimming pool at The Lakeland Forum. 3. Heritage Boat Association invited all Waterways Ireland personnel for a Cheese & Wine & Guinness reception prior to the Barbeque. 4. Later that evening NI Branch hosted a barbecue at the Round O. We got the weather-what a setting for a party!!!!!

SUNDAY 10TH AUGUST ; 1. 12.OO noon Ecumenical service at Round 'O quay. 2.00 pm Talk by Helen Elliott on the history of Enniskillen, Lough Erne and the surrounding area. In the evening, we joined together for a small BBQ. Sausages and Burgers with a bap!

Places for visiting this week.

  1. Shops- and lots of them in Enniskillen!!!!!!
  2. Devenish Island ; a must; don't come to the Erne without visiting Devenish. This is an old Monastic settlement with the famous Devenish Tower. No food available on island, toilet facilities beside small museum.
  3. Manor House; Hotel with leisure facilities, swimming pool etc. Across the lake is Inishmacsaint, another monastic settlement.
  4. Inishclare; Restaurant, safe harbour for 12 boats, water tours organised from here.
  5. Rossigh; Restaurant, good barbecue area, toilets etc.
  6. Castle Archdale Country Park; Bicycle Hire, Horse riding, Restaurant, Shop, lots of walks. There are a number of interesting islands off Castle Archdale, all with reasonable jetties. Don't miss White Island with the unique collection of stone figures.
  7. Lusty Beg Island; 70 acre island with water sports, 4x4 off road course etc.
  8. Kesh; Small town with some shopping, restaurants, etc. Jetties are in the river below bridge though it may be difficult to turn the barges.
  9. Castle Caldwell Country Park; Secluded area, good for barbecues and walks.

SATURDAY 16TH AUGUST; Belleek and District Development Association hosted a welcome for Heritage Boat Association including a visit to the world famous Belleek Pottery. HBA had a Barrel of Guinness to mark the occasion of barges travelling from Limerick to Belleek. Heritage Boat Association organised a pirate extravaganza!

SATURDAY 23RD AUGUST; Lough Erne Yacht Club, Goblusk Bay, Killadeas. The members of IWAI in NI met with us to say "BON VOYAGE".
